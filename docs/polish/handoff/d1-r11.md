## How tracing works (deviation from the brief, on purpose)
A 156 px pad is too small to trace on with a thumb, so the thumb draws its S anywhere, relative (like the old
"circle anywhere"). A position corridor failed any thumb S ±25% off the pad's size (tested), so the bead
follows by **heading**: a stroke within 45° of the S's heading slides the bead (waiting at corners on an
overshoot), an early turn skips ahead ≤100 px of arc, a stroke the S doesn't take there (off/back) costs
travel; 70 px of it = a miss. The thumb's S size is learnt per finished trace (gain). Either end starts (first
stroke picks; back-and-forth without lifting keeps alternating ends). Constants at the top of struggle.js.
