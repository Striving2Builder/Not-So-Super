## 4. Ranked fixes (cheapest browser version)

1. **Show the payoff on her body.** The car overhead and the chopper slung below are absent in every post-catch frame. Parent the target mesh to her hands for 2.5 s, hold the panel until it's on screen, and add a small FOV punch-in.
2. **Target in frame before the halo appears.** Spawn moments ahead inside the frustum at cruise height (the washer at her altitude; the car on a bridge, ramp or rooftop lot) instead of below the frame. Draw the halo only when the target is visible, otherwise show the edge arrow alone.
3. **Close the dead gaps.** Cap time between offers at about 15 s. Use cheap route filler: a hoop string, a slipstream ribbon, a pigeon flock to scatter, a smoke plume to swoop.
4. **One payoff grammar.** Every moment ends on an inked panel. Faller has no SAVED!, and the getaway ends with no visible outcome.
5. **Text diet.** While a moment is active, mute the MEANWHILE, closing-time and new-case toasts. Keep one caption lane at most.
6. **Kitten bug.** Normal scale, on a ledge or awning, with halo and arrow (pair 7).
7. **Keep the hero in frame on dives** (pair 4, impostor 3150). Widen FOV during a dive and keep her out from under the joystick zone.
8. **Speed in cruise without moving the rig.** Add an FOV kick on BOOST, screen-space speed lines, wind-streak particles, and a whoosh on near-passes of towers.
9. **Visible danger per moment.** A fraying rope or swinging rig, the car heading for a crowd or ledge, the chopper smoking and spinning. Add a ticking cue under 5 s.
10. **Impact frame on catch and tackle.** 3–4 frames of hit-stop, a white flash and a big POW/CAUGHT sticker at the contact point. None is visible in the impostor strip.
