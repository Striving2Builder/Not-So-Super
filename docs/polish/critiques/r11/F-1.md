## 3. Scores

- **vs AAA, raw:** 3/10
- **vs the best HTML5/WebGL games:** 6/10. The ink and halftone night city is above average for the web. The black-box bug, the invisible targets and the HUD pile-ups pull it under the bar.
- **Feel, from the strips:** 4/10
- **Verdict: NOT YET.** It got 1/8 pairs against the ≥6 needed, 6 against the 9 needed, and 4 against the 8.5 needed.

## 4. Ranked fixes (most impact first)

1. **Kill the black rectangle.** About 28% of the screen width at the bottom-right appears whenever a video panel is (or was) up: pair 7, the rings strip in every frame, getaway, faller, kitten, and about 8 commute frames. The LIVE panel also shows black at commute 126 s.
   - Cheapest: keep the panel container `display:none` / `opacity:0` until the video's `playing` event fires, give it a transparent background, and remove it on `ended`/`error`.
2. **Put the objective in the frame.** The car, the faller, the hoops and the kitten are never visible in any sampled frame. The camera stays fixed, so move the content instead.
   - Spawn every director moment inside a forward cone of ±35° on her current heading, 80–160 m out.
   - Route the getaway car across her heading, not behind her.
   - Drop the faller in front of her.
   - Lay hoops along her current vector at her current height.
   - Put the kitten on the nearest rooftop ahead.
   - Give each target a tall inked beacon pillar plus a 2x-scale black-outlined silhouette.
3. **Fill the commute with flight-native beats** every 10–15 s, ahead of her: an updraft ribbon, a 3-hoop mini-gate, a blimp, helicopter or bird flock to thread, a billboard to smash, and a "low skim" bonus between towers.
   - Show director offers as a glowing flare she flies into to accept. Ignoring it is the "fly on" choice, so the choice becomes visible.
   - Cheapest: reuse the existing hoop mesh and beacon shader, and spawn from a timer plus a heading check.
4. **Add speed juice.** No frame shows speed lines, FOV kick, cape stretch or ground rush, so cruising looks stationary.
   - Cheapest: a canvas or CSS radial speed-line overlay whose opacity scales with speed, +8° FOV lerp on BOOST, and a bigger cape-flutter amplitude. Changing FOV is a lens change, not a camera move.
5. **Make the HUD speak one at a time.** "Closing time" toasts stack on the objective banner (faller 3.1 s, kitten 2.8 s, rings 3 s), the LIVE panel overlaps the course banner (rings), and captions sit on the joystick ring.
   - Cheapest: a single toast queue with a 2.5 s minimum gap, flavour toasts muted while an event banner is active, and the caption anchored above the joystick zone.
6. **Land successes and failures.** The getaway loss just ends, the faller catch shows a cropped head, and the kitten never resolves.
   - Cheapest: a 1 s freeze plus a full-width comic panel ("ESCAPED!" / "SAVED!") with an ink burst. Place the rescued figure beside her at 60% of hero size, inside the frame.
7. **Fix the hero cruise pose and scale.**
   - The seated, knees-folded pose (pair 6/7, most of the commute) and the arm-over-head rear pose (pair 8) read as broken.
   - She covers about 20–25% of the centre frame, right where targets would appear.
   - Cheapest: one streamlined pose (fist forward, legs together, slight body roll on turns), 15% smaller, and a consistent inverted-hull outline instead of the ragged one.
8. **Stop the wall-hugging.** In kitten 13 s, faller 8.5 s and pair 3 a flat window-tile facade fills more than half the frame.
   - Cheapest: soft lateral repulsion from facades inside 8 m, plus dithered fade of any building between the camera and the hero.
9. **Make captions point somewhere.** Replace the repeated "not a crook in sight" lines with directional bait tied to the next offer, e.g. "SMOKE OVER THE FARM DISTRICT, 300 m, YOUR 2 O'CLOCK!".
   - Cheapest: dedupe the pool and template it from the nearest offer's bearing and distance.
10. **Give the daytime city texture.** Daytime Downtown is the same window tile on every tower (pairs 1, 3, 8; kitten), while the night grade (pair 6, commute) is far stronger.
    - Cheapest: bias travel toward dusk/night, scatter instanced rooftop props (water towers, AC units, antennas), add 3–4 neon sign sprites per block, and a vertex-darkened band at the base of each building for fake AO.
