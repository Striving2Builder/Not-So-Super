## 4. Ranked fixes (cheapest browser version)
1. **Gate beats by twist.** The snapper standoff should only appear when the photo is real. Setup gets a break-out-of-the-ring beat, planted gets a CCTV-booth beat, and lookalike gets a find-the-double chase. *Cheap:* tag each existing beat with its twists and write 1 new beat per twist in the same dialog UI.
2. **Make the tabloid photo the actual gotcha.** Grab the WebGL canvas (`toDataURL`) at the selfie or flash moment and use it as the front-page image. Remove the green circle. Write 6 or more headline and lede templates keyed to the twist and the clue. *Cheap:* one canvas grab plus a string table.
3. **Stage the scandal in 3D, not in text boxes.** Show phones up around her (billboard phone quads plus white flash sprites), a glass mesh in her hand, and the snapper running with a trail. *Cheap:* sprites plus a 0.5 s camera push on each beat.
4. **Keep the heroine readable in the crowd** (pairs 1, 7, 8). Draw her a second time with no depth test as a flat ink silhouette or rim, and swap the dither band for per-figure fade. *Cheap:* a second material pass on her mesh only.
5. **Give each venue its own identity.** Use a per-zone fog and key-light colour, crowd rim tint, and one landmark prop visible from spawn (a UV wash, a CCTV wall, a VIP rope). *Cheap:* about 3 numbers and 1 prop per zone.
6. **Show the proofs as objects.** Zoom in on a napkin mesh, an X-ray reveal of the knockoff suit, or a CCTV monitor playing the clip, instead of a "Proof / Continue" card. *Cheap:* a textured plane plus a camera dolly.
7. **Write variants of the beats that repeat in every visit.** Each suspect should push in their own way: Benny with bottles, Dee Dee by livestreaming, Vince by staging the double. Give the "one shot" and "say cheese" beats 3 variants each. *Cheap:* text only.
8. **Make choices land visually.** Push through should make bodies stagger. Jump should launch her over the ring. Palm it should show a super-speed blur. *Cheap:* canned tweens plus a screen shake.
9. **Stretch the arc.** Require 2 or 3 clues or an accusation before the headline, so a visit is not a 16 s rail. *Cheap:* gate the proof on the clue count.
10. **UI hygiene.** Speech bubbles cover the LIVE feed (gen5 and gen8), and an empty black panel sits under the video. "HIS ENVELOPE: 0" is cryptic. Reserve a lane on the left for bubbles and hide empty panels.
