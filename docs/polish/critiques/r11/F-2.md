## 4. Ranked fixes (cheapest browser-feasible version)
1. **Put the target in frame.** Car, washer, heli and impostor are invisible in 4 of the 5 strips. Cheapest: during "in reach", widen FOV and pitch the lens toward the target (rig untouched). Scale targets about 1.5× with a thick ink outline plus a pulsing halo, and add a screen-edge arrow when off-screen.
2. **Stage the payoff on screen.** Use about 300 ms of hit-stop plus slow-mo on the grab, and show the result: car lifted, washer in her arms, wig flying. Then show the comic panel. Replace the impostor's modal "Continue" dialog with an UNMASKED! panel that auto-dismisses.
3. **One message lane while a moment is active.** Queue toasts, mute the district caption and news ticker, and keep beacon icons off the objective banner. Captions must not clip at the screen edge or cover the joystick.
4. **Show the threat before the clock starts.** Make the snap, fall or swerve visible first. The washer should visibly slide down the façade as the timer runs, and the car should head for a crosswalk with pedestrians. Allow one miss and a recovery so a moment has more than one beat.
5. **Sell speed.** Add comic radial speed lines on boost, an FOV kick, wind streaks, and bright skim sparks or a "+style" pop when she passes close to buildings. Spider-Man's dive (pair 8) is the bar.
6. **Fill the gaps.** Offer the next moment within about 6–8 s of the last one resolving. Seed ambient micro-goals on the route: loose hoops, updraft or slipstream lanes, a civilian waving from a roof.
7. **Lengthen and chain moments.** Rings took 6.5 s, the car about 2.6 s, the faller about 2 s. Build 2–3 stage versions, e.g. the car swerves and the hoops weave between towers.
8. **Fix the rescued-civilian lump** on rooftops (pairs 5 and 6 and the faller strip). Use a standing, waving pose and a THANKS! bubble. Make the speech-bubble timing match the state.
9. **Dress flat surfaces.** The bare roofs and repeating night window tiles need halftone or ink hatching on roofs, plus a few rooftop props (AC units, water towers) via instancing. Find the cause of the black-void frame (commute, 48 s).
10. **Touch-first hints.** Swap "▲ / R" and "▼ / F" for a pulse on the on-screen up/down button.
