## 3. Scores

- **vs AAA (raw): 3/10**
- **vs the best HTML5/WebGL games: 6.5/10**. The crowd count, Supergirl's model and the danger grammar (cone, ! burst, red edge, stamps) beat most browser games.
- **Feel: 5.5/10**. The spot and grab beats work. Idle and X-ray are flat, and the foreground dither undercuts every calm frame.
- **Verdict: NOT YET**

## 4. Ranked fixes (cheapest browser version)

1. **Kill the foreground screen-door dither.** It reads as a glitch, not comic ink (pairs 1, 7, 8; idle). Cheapest fix: bodies between the camera and her become a flat dark ink silhouette at about 35% alpha with an outline. Limit the halftone to a small ring around her, and hide any mesh within ~2 m of the camera (this also fixes the giant bouncer head in idle).
2. **Make bouncers read as threats.** Swap the ball head for one with a shades decal and earpiece, use a black tee with SECURITY in white, and scale him to 1.15×. Keep the stripe pattern only if it is a deliberate comic gag.
3. **Show the grab.** Parent two hand meshes to her upper-arm bone during GRABBED. Run a real 0.3 s timeScale dip plus an FOV push and vignette. Cut BUSTED! and show one stamp per beat.
4. **Protect chase tension.** While alert > 0, suppress hear/clue bubbles and TIPs. Add a heartbeat pulse on the red edge and shorten the time to LOST THEM! to about 6–8 s once she's out of sight.
5. **Make idle escalate.** Shrink the ring radius over time, raise the flash rate, show a "That's HER!" line at about 5 s, and at about 8 s turn the nearest bouncer's head with a ?. Readable doom, but she can always leave.
6. **Fix X-ray.** Darken the crowd instead of deleting it. Make the glyph chips 2× larger with icon plus colour. Clamp the LEAD beacon to a safe inset away from the screen edge and the HUD.
7. **Use a real CCTV cut.** Render a 128 px feed of her from a second camera with a REC overlay. Failing that, use a baked sprite of her in a green night-vision tint. Static noise sells nothing.
8. **Queue onboarding text.** Show one tutorial, TIP or NEWS FLASH at a time, each for at least 1.2 s. Fix the ghost text line in the SHAKEN! frame.
9. **Back the HUD.** Give the left stats panel an 85% opaque fill. It washes out over bright LED walls in pairs 1, 5 and 7.
10. **Fix camera facing at interactables.** At the DJ booth, offset the camera to a three-quarter view so her face and the booth both read. Today she shows her back into a wall of heads.
