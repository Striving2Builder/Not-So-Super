## 3. Scores

- vs AAA raw: **2.5/10**
- vs the best HTML5/WebGL games: **6/10**. The stylised neon stage, toon rim lines and dense crowd count are above average for the web. The faceless crowd, empty floor and UI pile-ups are below the best.
- Feel from the strips: **5.5/10**. The loop has the right verbs (cone, crowd cover, takedown, X-ray, struggle). The key beats (spotted, grabbed, escaped) land as text and HUD changes rather than as camera, light and sound-image events.
- **Verdict: NOT YET** (0/8 pairs, 6/10 vs best HTML5, 5.5 feel)

## 4. Ranked fixes

1. **Clear the near-camera crowd.** This affects pair 3, pair 7, pair 8 and the stealth strip at 5300-9150 ms. Cheapest version: in the crowd instanced shader, screen-door dither (Bayer discard) any instance whose screen position is within about 120 px of the hero, or closer to the camera than the hero. It is one uniform plus one discard, with no transparency sorting.
2. **Give the crowd variety and life.** Cheapest version: a per-instance colour from an 8-tone palette (lift albedo above near-black), 3-4 head and hair-cap variants swapped by instance ID, a small random scale and lean, and a phase offset on the arm bob. Add a pale face disc on the camera-facing side for nearer instances only.
3. **Fix the floor.** The black void floor covers 30-40% of every frame. Cheapest version: one 256 px canvas texture of LED tiles, tinted by the beat and UV-scrolled. Add additive light-pool quads under each spotlight, plus a blob contact shadow under every character (one shared texture, instanced quad), which also fixes the floating downed bouncer.
4. **Fix the cape.** In pairs 2, 7 and 8 it points straight up. Clamp the cape bone or vertex pitch to about 70 degrees and add damping. This is a few lines and is the most visible bug in the set.
5. **Make "spotted" a full-screen event.** Cheapest version: on "!", drop all ambient bubbles and tips, take 0.4 s of 0.6x time scale, a 6% camera FOV punch, a thick red edge vignette (one fullscreen quad), and cut the stage screen texture to a desaturated, scanlined render target of her for 3 s. Add a "LOST THEM" stamp when line of sight is broken.
6. **Show the grab in-world before the panel.** Cheapest version: 0.5 s of camera push onto the bouncer's hand-on-arm pose, a white flash, then the panel. On the panel, put the body text on a dark plate below the bar, and replace the loss spoiler with one line of stakes.
7. **Put the HUD on a diet.** Fix the "GET CLOSER" banner overlapping the title card. Show only one transient layer at a time (news flash, tip or bubble). Cut tips to one line, and collapse the left stat panel (about 16% x 30% of the screen) to two slim bars that fade when idle.
8. **De-clutter X-ray.** Show tags only for the nearest 3 guards, push them apart vertically by screen-space overlap, and use an icon plus colour instead of "CALM - TORCH" text. Light the target clue with a pulsing beacon visible during X-ray so the 1700-3700 ms dead air has a destination.
9. **Add a cheap bloom.** Use a quarter-resolution threshold and blur pass applied to the stage screen, neons and spotlight cones only (layer mask), plus a fog gradient to separate the depth bands. This closes much of the lighting gap to pairs 1 and 4.
10. **Dress the props.** The DJ booth needs bevelled edges, an emissive trim strip, decks and laptop boxes on top, and the "NIGHTCLUB" sign lifted above the hero's head height. These are a handful of boxes and one emissive material.
