1. SETUP (do this first)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

STYLE
  Modern comic-book line art: clean ink outlines, cel-shaded flat colours,
  atmospheric lighting. (The "rainy rooftops" / "grey city rooftop" look.)

STYLE (written into every prompt)
  Each prompt already describes the style in full, so it works on its own:
  "Contemporary comic-book illustration in a polished graphic-novel style: bold clean black ink outlines of varying line weight, cel-shaded flat colour blocks with smooth soft gradients, light ink hatching in the shadows, a muted, slightly desaturated palette tinted by the scene lighting described below, with warm light accents, gentle atmospheric haze for depth, crisp readable shapes, clean digital comic finish, no painterly blur, no photorealism."
  You don't need a style reference image. For extra consistency you CAN
  attach your best finished scene as a reference too, optional.
  Do NOT use watermarked artist images (e.g. the DeviantArt one) as a
  reference.

SIZE
  5:3 aspect ratio, about 2000 x 1200 pixels.
  Midjourney: add   --ar 5:3 --no people, text, letters, watermark
                    (optional: --sref <link to your best finished scene>)
  ChatGPT / Gemini: paste the prompt as-is.

RULES EVERY IMAGE MUST FOLLOW (already written into each prompt)
  - Straight-on, eye-level view like a theatre stage set; back wall faces
    the camera.
  - The line where wall meets floor sits about 63% of the way down.
  - Every prop is a separate, clearly readable object - nothing overlapping.
    (Each prop becomes something the player taps.)
  - Empty floor in the lower-right quarter (a witness stands there).
  - No people. No readable text, signs or lettering.
  - Props do not need exact positions - roughly left-to-right as listed is
    enough. I will move the tap areas to match your image.

MOOD ADD-ONS (optional, append to a prompt)
  Neon districts:   vivid neon signage glow, wet reflective surfaces.
  Red Light scenes: dominant crimson colour grade.

7. FIXES WHEN AN IMAGE COMES OUT WRONG
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Room is tilted / 3D-looking      add: "flat frontal view, one-point perspective,
                                        camera parallel to the back wall"
  Props merged or overlapping      add: "generous space between each object"
  Text or letters appear           add: "all signs, posters and papers blank",
                                        then regenerate
  Style drifts from other scenes   attach your best finished scene as a style
                                   reference (Midjourney: --sref <link>)
  People appear                    add: "empty scene, no figures, no silhouettes"
  Too dark to see props            add: "props clearly lit and readable"
